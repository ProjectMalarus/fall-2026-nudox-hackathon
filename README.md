# fall-2026-nudox-hackathon

This is my submission for the Nudox track in the KSU AI Club Hackathon

How to play:

Click on a suspect to chat to them.

Type your questions in the chatbox.

Once you feel confident in your answer or run out of questions make your accusation by pressing the accuse button on the suspect's card.

Hit 'Play Again' to play a new round with a new case and different suspects. There are 3 cases in total.
